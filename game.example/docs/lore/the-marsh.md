# Greywater Marsh

North of Thornhollow the ground stops committing. The causeway holds for the
first half-mile and then becomes a suggestion, and after that the difference
between the path and the water is a matter of opinion and depth.

## Why anyone goes

- The reeds are worth money in the village, and worth more in the season when
  nobody sensible is cutting them.
- Things get lost out here and stay lost, which means things that were lost a
  long time ago are still where they fell.
- Something in the deep water is old enough to be interesting to people who
  study that sort of thing from a safe distance.

## Why most people do not

The marsh is patient in a way that a fight is not. Nothing out here needs to
beat you in a round. It only needs you to still be standing in the same place
in an hour, which — if you are the sort of person who stops to look at things —
you probably will be.
