# Thornhollow

The village sits in a bowl of land the road has to descend into and climb out
of, which is most of what there is to say about why it is still here. Traffic
that would rather not stop has no choice about slowing down.

## The square

Everything that matters is one turn from the square. The smith keeps the forge
lit past dark because the mine pays for it, the general store buys anything and
sells most of it back, and the notice board is where the village argues with
itself in writing.

## Out of the village

- **The mine**, east and down. It collapsed once and was reopened by people who
  needed it more than they feared it.
- **The marsh**, north along the old causeway. Nobody in Thornhollow will go
  with you and several of them will tell you why.
- **The crypt**, which the village does not discuss and does not lock.

### A word about the marsh

The causeway is the only dry way in and it is not reliably dry. Whatever is
living out there has had a long time to get used to the idea that visitors
arrive one at a time, on a known path, in daylight.
