# Stances

A stance is how you are standing, not what you are doing. It costs nothing to
adopt, applies until you change it or fall over, and it is the cheapest way to
tell a fight what you want out of it.

## Choosing one

Nobody in Thornhollow will teach you a stance you cannot hold. The guard at the
gate will spar with you for free, which is the fastest way to find out which of
these suits the weapon you are carrying.

- **Balanced** — no modifier in either direction. This is what you are in when
  you have not chosen, and it is a perfectly good answer.
- **Aggressive** — you hit harder and more often, and so does everything
  swinging at you. Good against something slower than you are.
- **Defensive** — the mirror. Your rounds get longer and your defences get
  better. Good against a crowd, and good when you are waiting for a heal.

### On changing mid-fight

Changing stance does not cost a round, but it does not refund one either. If you
are already recovering from a swing, the new stance applies to the swing after
this one — the round you are in was priced when it started.

## What a stance is not

A stance is not a substitute for armour, and it is not a substitute for leaving.
An aggressive stance against something that outclasses you converts a long
losing fight into a short one.
